# RBI lending scope

The product monitors and answers questions only from official Reserve Bank of India
material relevant to lending. The initial corpus and daily monitoring scope cover digital
lending, fair lending and penal charges, borrower disclosures and Key Facts Statements,
personal-loan pricing and resets, lending restrictions, NBFC lending, co-lending, recovery
practices, and the prudential treatment of advances.

The system excludes unrelated RBI domains such as monetary policy, payments, foreign
exchange, and general banking material unless it directly changes a lending requirement.
Every answer must remain linked to the RBI page or document chunk that supports it.

## Curated sources

The monitored registry contains broad RBI discovery pages and individual cornerstone
lending documents. Individual pages are monitored so a revision remains detectable even
when it drops off a listing page. The initial curated set includes the Digital Lending
Directions, penal-charges guidance, floating-rate personal-loan directions, and prudential
norms for advances. The catalogue should grow through reviewed additions, not unrestricted
web crawling.
